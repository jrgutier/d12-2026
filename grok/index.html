How the sheet was built

League: D12 - Round 14. Format: 12-team Superflex auction, $200 budget. Scoring: Half-PPR, 5-pt pass TD, 0.05 pass yards, 0.2 rush-attempt bonus. Roster: QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, TE, 2 FLEX, SUPER_FLEX, DEF, 5 BN. Weeks 1–14 regular season (H2H + vs median). 6-team playoffs, weeks 15–17, byes for seeds 1–2, re-seed.

Each player’s week-by-week 2026 line comes from Sleeper (Rotowire) and is scored with this league’s exact settings, including the rush-attempt bonus and 5-point passing touchdowns. A missing week is a real bye or a part-season (Cousins weeks 1–2, Mendoza from week 3, Jacobs missing week 1). Lineups are re-optimized every week. Player weeks are then sampled with position-typical variance, and 8,000 full seasons are played out against a round-robin schedule plus the median extra game.

This is a draft-only snapshot as of 2026-09-07. Waivers, trades, and extra injuries on top of the weekly lines are not modeled. Several quarterback jobs are projection bets: Daniel Jones, Malik Willis, Tyler Shough, Jaxson Dart. If those jobs move, the board moves with them.
