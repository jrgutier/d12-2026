The case

The savings went into the three players who actually fill the board — James Cook, Kenneth Walker, and Puka Nacua — then into the glue that wins versus-median weeks: Mike Evans at $4, Parker Washington at $5, Rhamondre Stevenson at $3. You start nine people in this league. This is one of the only rosters that actually has nine.

The hole

If that job disappears, Superflex slides to a flex running back and the title odds compress. Rodgers is not a plan. Until that job opens up, this is the favorite.

Path to a title. Survive Week 7 without a 0–2, ride Cook/Puka, and let Jones be boring.

How the sheet was built

League: D12 - Round 14. Format: 12-team Superflex auction, $200 budget. Scoring: Half-PPR, 5-pt pass TD, 0.05 pass yards, 0.2 rush-attempt bonus. Roster: QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, TE, 2 FLEX, SUPER_FLEX, DEF, 5 BN. Weeks 1–14 regular season (H2H + vs median). 6-team playoffs, weeks 15–17, byes for seeds 1–2, re-seed.

Each player is scored from Sleeper’s 2026 season projections using this league’s exact settings, including the rush-attempt bonus and 5-point passing touchdowns. Weekly lineups are re-optimized every week (QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, TE, 2 FLEX, SUPER_FLEX, DEF) with bye weeks zeroed out. Player weeks are sampled with position-typical variance, then 8,000 full seasons are played out against a round-robin schedule plus the median extra game.

This is a draft-only snapshot as of 2026-09-06. Waivers, trades, and injuries after week 1 are not modeled. Several quarterback jobs are projection bets: the model starts Daniel Jones, Malik Willis, Tyler Shough, and Jaxson Dart because that is how Sleeper projects them today. If those jobs move, the board moves with them — especially #1 Fist My Bump (Jones) and #6 Let Lindsay Clancy Walk (Willis).
